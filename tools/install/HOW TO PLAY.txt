Sea Power Multiplayer - co-op campaign fork, TEST BUILD 3a
=========================================================

INSTALL (every player - up to 4)
  1. Unzip this whole folder anywhere.
  2. Double-click "Install (double-click me).bat".
  3. If it warns about the Workshop copy of Seapower Multiplayer, unsubscribe
     from it in Steam. Everyone must use THIS build.
  Everyone also needs the campaign mod the host plays, same version:
  Bungalow's "Dynamic Campaign Mod" and/or "Dynamic Campaign Mod (fork)"
  (Pacific 2027). The mod matching below fetches them for you.

MATCHING MODS (automatic)
  When the guest joins, the guest's game makes its mods match the host's:
  it subscribes to and downloads Workshop mods the guest is missing, updates
  older ones, and ticks/unticks mods in the mod list in the host's order.
  When it's done it says so - then the guest restarts Sea Power and rejoins.
  Mods that aren't on the Workshop are named so the host can send them.
  Turn off: [Mods] MatchHostMods = false (or TurnOffModsHostDoesntHave = false
  to only add what's missing).

PLAY A DYNAMIC CAMPAIGN TOGETHER (either mod)
  1. Host: load your campaign (Bungalow's campaigns or Pacific 2027).
  2. Host: Ctrl+F9 -> Host, invite up to 3 players through Steam (Invite Player).
  3. Guest: accept. The host's campaign loads on your PC automatically
     (loading screen). If it doesn't: open any campaign of the same mod, then Ctrl+F6.
  4. The host's game runs the campaign; the guest's map follows it.
  - Map drawings, convoy/supply priorities and refit queues are shared too.
  - The host decides "fight or auto-resolve" when a battle comes up.

COMMANDING
  - Everything starts SHARED: anyone can order any task force/flight.
  - Select a task force or flight on the campaign map: a box in the bottom
    left shows who commands it, with a Claim / Release button (Ctrl+F7 does
    the same). A claimed one only its owner can order.
  - Claimed units are coloured on both maps: host amber, then cyan, green and pink for players 2-4
    (the same colours as your mouse cursors).
  - Guest orders go to the host's game; you'll see them a second later.
  - Everyone can do everything: move fleets, missions, prepare and
    launch flights, form/split task forces, shipyard and Admiralty orders,
    refits, invasions. Things the guest creates appear at once on their screen
    and are confirmed by the host's game a second later.
  - Ctrl+F6 (guest): reload the host's campaign if your map looks wrong.
  - Positions, routes, points, renown, rank and bases update every second;
    stocks, intel, reports, garrisons and the front every 5 seconds. Ship
    fuel refreshes after each battle (or Ctrl+F6).

SEEING EACH OTHER
  - Everyone else's mouse shows on the campaign map, and in battles only on
    the tactical map while it's on screen. Off in battles:
    [Campaign] PartnerCursorsInBattle = false. Off everywhere: PartnerCursors = false.
  - When the host's campaign starts a battle, it's sent to the guest
    automatically. The guest starts with the task forces they claimed;
    the host has the rest and can send formations over as usual.
  - After the battle the guest is brought back to the campaign map.

IF SOMETHING BREAKS
  Send BepInEx\LogOutput.log from EVERY PC (in the Sea Power folder),
  plus a line on what you were doing.

UNINSTALL
  Quit Sea Power, then double-click "Uninstall (double-click me).bat".
  It removes the mod and its temporary files, and asks before deleting your
  settings. Your other mods, campaigns and saves are left alone.
